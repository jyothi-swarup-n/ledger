## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an uncompromising, technical, and high-velocity aesthetic engineered for personal finance tracking. By merging the structural candor of neo-brutalism with an ultra-refined dark terminal ethos, the interface reframes budgeting from an anxious chore into an empowering, data-driven discipline.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The UI exudes precision, kinetic energy, and definitive authority. Every container asserts its boundary with architectural clarity; there are no ambiguous gradients, amorphous drop shadows, or soft blurred surfaces. Financial metrics are presented with unapologetic focus, fostering an emotional state of hyper-competence, direct control, and instantaneous comprehension.

### Design Movement
**Dark Neo-Brutalism:** High-contrast structural boundaries, tactile hard offset drop shadows, deep graphite containers, and saturated neon accents. Structural containment is prioritized through deliberate line work (1.5px to 2px solid strokes), hard rectilinear planes with subtly radiused corners, and sharp categorical color coding.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an ink-rich, deep-space canvas punctuated by functional high-voltage neons. Color operates strictly as information architecture: directing attention, establishing visual hierarchy, and communicating financial status with absolute clarity.

### Color Tokens & Roles

- **Canvas & Base (`#0b0e14`):** Deep pitch-slate ground. Used for screen backgrounds and master page containers.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#141824`):** Primary card, sheet, and structural block background.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1c2234`):** Elevated segments, interactive row highlights, and nested data modules.
- **Structural Border (`#2a334a`):** Crisp, unwavering boundary definition for all dark surfaces.
- **Primary Accent / Electric Lime (`#ccff00`):** Positive delta, critical call-to-actions, active state toggles, and primary net-worth telemetry. Paired strictly with `#0b0e14` text for maximum contrast.
- **Secondary Accent / Vibrant Cobalt (`#3865ff`):** Investment yields, transfers, utility metrics, and interactive selections.
- **Tertiary Accent / Punchy Coral (`#ff4757`):** Expense overages, debt counters, liquidation alerts, and negative transaction deltas.
- **Telemetry Emerald (`#10b981`) & Cyan (`#00f2fe`):** Cash flow velocity indicators, savings target fills, and multi-asset chart vectors.
- **Typography - Primary (`#ffffff`):** Pure white for headline totals, currency denominations, and critical data points.
- **Typography - Secondary / Label (`#94a3b8`):** Muted slate for metadata, subheaders, timestamps, and inactive field labels.
- **Typography - Inverse (`#0b0e14`):** Solid pitch black applied directly over high-chroma neon badges and primary buttons.

## Typography

Space Grotesk serves as the singular typographic voice across display, transactional data, and system labels. Its idiosyncratic ink-traps and mechanical geometry provide a raw terminal quality that reinforces structural honesty.

### Numerical Treatment
All numerical readouts, account balances, and ticker amounts must enable tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal layout shift during dynamic counter animations and balance updates.

### Hierarchy & Case
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Bold, negative tracking (-0.02em to -0.04em), tight line-heights. Reserved for net asset aggregates and top-level summary totals.
- **Labels & Tags:** Uppercase by default with deliberate positive letter-spacing (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`) to guarantee quick peripheral scanning against dark backgrounds.
- **Body & Metadata:** Regular to Medium weights. Secondary copy uses `#94a3b8` to maintain contrast ratios exceeding WCAG AAA requirements against `#141824`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a rigid 4px/8px incremental grid. Every module locks into visual alignment using solid borders, avoiding loose whitespace in favor of tightly coordinated data densities.

### Responsive Behavior & Grid System
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column stack governed by a 4-column layout matrix with `16px` outer screen margins and `12px` component gutters. Cards span the full usable width. Horizontal carousels for card tiers snap with a strict 12px offset.
- **Tablet / Expanded (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `24px` margins and `16px` gutters. Analytical graphs and spend-category matrices split into equal dual-column spans (4 columns each).
- **Rhythm & Structure:** Padding inside cards remains invariant: `16px` vertical and `16px` horizontal for dense feeds, stepping up to `20px` for hero account cards. Elements never touch the structural frame without a minimum `8px` internal gutter.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects soft gaussian blurs, translucent frosted glass, and feathered drop shadows. Visual depth is created through **Tactile Offset Shadows** and **Tonal Container Stacking**.

### Tactile Offset Shadows
Elevated components cast a hard, un-blurred offset shadow directly downward and to the right. 
- **Resting Elevation (Base Cards):** `3px 3px 0px #05070a` with a `1.5px solid #2a334a` border.
- **Interactive / High Elevation (Modals, Active Cards):** `5px 5px 0px #05070a` with a `2px solid #2a334a`.
- **Neon Interactive Elevation (Primary CTA, Active Badges):** `4px 4px 0px #ccff00` behind a solid `#141824` container, or `4px 4px 0px #05070a` behind an active `#ccff00` filled container.
- **Pressed State:** The hard shadow snaps to `0px 0px 0px`, translating the element `+3px` on the X-axis and `+3px` on the Y-axis to simulate a physical mechanical switch.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Floor (`#0b0e14`):** Canvas background.
2. **Tier 1 (`#141824`):** Ledger groups, transaction lists, analytical envelopes.
3. **Tier 2 (`#1c2234`):** Individual transaction rows within a group, nested budget meters, search bars.
4. **Floating (`#1c2234`):** Sticky action bars, action sheets, and balance drawers outlined in high-contrast `#3865ff` or `#2a334a`.

## Shapes

The design system employs controlled, compact corner geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Radii are restricted to subtle architectural bevels (`4px` base, `8px` for larger cards, `12px` maximum for sheets) to preserve the sharp, engineered aesthetic of industrial terminals.

- **Base Controls & Badges:** `4px` corner radius.
- **Cards, Modules, & Containers:** `8px` corner radius.
- **Overlays & Full Bottom Sheets:** `12px` corner radius on top edges only.
- **Data Toggles & Switches:** Strict rectangular housings (`4px` radius) containing rectangular thumb switches; no pill-shaped toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Button:** Filled with Electric Lime (`#ccff00`), text in Pitch Black (`#0b0e14`, `label-lg`, uppercase). 2px solid border in `#ccff00`. Hard offset shadow: `4px 4px 0px #000000`. Active state: `translate(2px, 2px)` with reduced `2px 2px 0px` shadow.
- **Secondary Structural Button:** Background `#141824`, border `1.5px solid #2a334a`, text `#ffffff`. Shadow: `3px 3px 0px #05070a`. Hover/Focus border shifts to `#3865ff`.
- **Destructive Button:** Background `#141824`, border `1.5px solid #ff4757`, text `#ff4757`. Hard shadow: `3px 3px 0px #ff4757`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact rectangular frames (`4px` radius) with `1px solid` matching the text color. Uppercase `label-sm`.
  - *Income / Surplus:* Border `#ccff00`, background `rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.1)`, text `#ccff00`.
  - *Expense / Debit:* Border `#ff4757`, background `rgba(255, 71, 87, 0.1)`, text `#ff4757`.
  - *Category Tags:* Border `#2a334a`, background `#1c2234`, text `#94a3b8`.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Solid `#141824` background, `2px solid #2a334a`, `4px` radius, padding `12px 16px`. Text is Space Grotesk `body-lg` in `#ffffff`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions instantaneously to `#ccff00`, with a hard accent shadow `3px 3px 0px #ccff00`. Placeholder text in `#94a3b8`.
- **Error State:** Border becomes `#ff4757` with a `3px 3px 0px #ff4757` hard shadow; helper label renders in bold `#ff4757`.

### Transaction Lists
- **Item Row:** Contained within `#141824`, separated by `1.5px solid #2a334a` dividers or stacked as discrete cards with `8px` margins. 
- **Leading Indicator:** High-contrast `36x36px` square (`4px` radius) icon block with `#1c2234` ground and accent borders denoting category (e.g., `#3865ff` for subscriptions, `#ccff00` for deposits).
- **Right Alignment:** Primary monetary figure in `headline-sm`, tabular numbers, styled `#ffffff` for debits or `#ccff00` for credits.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Checkbox:** `20x20px` square, `2px solid #2a334a` base. When checked: background `#ccff00`, border `#ccff00`, displaying an angular `#0b0e14` geometric checkmark.
- **Radio Selector:** `20x20px` square (neo-brutalist alternative to circles), `2px solid #2a334a`. Active state features a solid `10x10px` inner square in `#3865ff` with a `#3865ff` perimeter.

### Cards & Analytical Containers
- **Account Summary Card:** Background `#141824`, border `2px solid #2a334a`, `8px` corner radius, shadow `4px 4px 0px #05070a`. Interior modules framed in `#1c2234` with `1px solid #2a334a`.
- **Budget Progress Meters:** Heavy rectangular tracks (`#1c2234` ground, `1.5px solid #2a334a`) holding non-rounded, segmented progress fills in `#ccff00` (safe) stepping to `#ff4757` (breached).
- **Telemetry Sparklines:** Crisp `2px` vector polyline with sharp zero-radius vertices. No area gradient fills beneath the line; pure linear signal in `#00f2fe` or `#ccff00`.
